Option 1

Hotel Riu Guanacaste

from $1,127 total

Beachfront on Culebra Bay, with the mountains rising green behind the property. This is the all-family, always-something-on version of all-inclusive: a water park (Splash Water World), a swim-up bar at the main pool, a nightclub, and a kids' program if anyone in your group is bringing little ones. It's the busier of the two options — lively rather than quiet.

Option 2

The Westin Reserva Conchal, an All-Inclusive Golf Resort & Spa

Set inside a golf and nature reserve near Playa Conchal, this is the quieter, more grown-up version of all-inclusive — family-friendly still, but built around the golf course, the spa, and a shell-sand beach rather than a water park. Complimentary parking is included if anyone's renting a car.
