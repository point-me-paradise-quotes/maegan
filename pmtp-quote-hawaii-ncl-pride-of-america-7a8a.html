Norwegian Pride of America

The only ship that sails round-trip from Honolulu, every week of the year.

Most Hawaii cruises spend four or five days crossing open ocean from the mainland. Pride of America skips all of that. She’s US-flagged with an all-American crew — a rarity in cruising — which is what lets her sail Hawaii-only itineraries with no foreign port stop. The result is a week where every single day is an island day: the ship overnights in both Maui and Kauai, so you get evenings ashore that other cruises can’t offer, and your final evening is spent cruising the cliffs of the Na Pali Coast — a stretch of Kauai you can otherwise only reach by boat, helicopter, or an 11-mile trail.

The ship herself: 922 feet, 11 decks, room for 2,180 guests with a crew of 927. She entered service in 2005 and came out of her most recent refurbishment in May 2025. Onboard you’ll find two main dining rooms, a buffet and several casual cafés and grills, Broadway- and Vegas-style theater shows, eight pools plus hot tubs, a fitness center, and a full run of bars and lounges.

There are no traditional sea days on this route. The ship overnights in Maui and Kauai — so you can watch the sun go down from shore, have a real dinner off the ship, and be back at your own pace — and every other day is a port call. Day 7’s Na Pali cruising is scenic only (the ship doesn’t land), which is exactly how you want to see those 3,000-foot sea cliffs anyway.

Free at Sea — Carried by Your Fare

NCL’s bundled package rides along with your cruise fare. Here’s what it covers for both of you.

  • Unlimited Open Bar — All cocktails (100+ on the menu), premium spirits including Grey Goose, Casamigos, and Woodford Reserve, plus wine and beer by the glass, sodas, and juices. Applies to guests 21+. You also get 20% off bottles of wine and champagne, and no corkage fee if you bring your own bottle aboard.
  • Specialty Dining — 3 meals each — On a 7-night sailing, you each get three dinners at the specialty restaurants: Cagney’s Steakhouse, La Cucina (Italian), or Jefferson’s Bistro (French). Each meal includes up to 3 appetizers and desserts alongside your entrée. Book before you sail through your NCL account or app — Cagney’s fills first.
  • Wi-Fi — 150 minutes each — High-speed Starlink Wi-Fi, one login per person, one device at a time.
  • Shore Excursion Credit — $50 per port — $50 off every excursion booked through NCL, applied to the first guest on the reservation. With five port stops, that’s up to $250 in credits.

What still costs extra

  • Gratuities — $20 per person per day plus Hawaii’s 4.275% GET tax ($291.96 total for your sailing) — pre-payable below, or charged onboard
  • Extra specialty dinners beyond your three each — cover charges apply
  • Top-shelf drinks outside the open bar (the “Connoisseur Collection” — Macallan 12, Patrón Añejo, etc.) — you get $15 off retail with your package
  • Starbucks drinks and bottled water — not in the standard package
  • Wi-Fi beyond your 150 minutes — upgrade packages available
  • Excursion cost beyond the $50 credit per port
  • A 20% gratuity applies to any bar or dining purchases outside your package
Fine print worth knowing: Your Free at Sea selection locks in at least 24 hours before sailing and can’t be changed onboard. Everyone in the stateroom carries the same package. Anything consumed beyond the package is charged at prevailing rates.
