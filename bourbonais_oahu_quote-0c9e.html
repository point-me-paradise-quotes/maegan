Hotel La Croix

Two room types are quoted at this property — compare them below alongside Hilton and Sheraton.

Hotel La Croix sits beside two parks in Waikiki, named for the neighborhood's history as a retreat for Hawaiian royal families. The design leans on European marble and native hardwoods, and rooms look out toward the Pacific, the mountains, or the Waikiki skyline depending on which side of the building you're on.
