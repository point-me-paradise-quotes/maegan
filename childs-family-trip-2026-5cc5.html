The stay

A week off the gift-wrap grind, traded for a room facing the water.

Hotel RIU Guanacaste sits directly on the beach in the Guanacaste region, on Costa Rica's Pacific side. The property finished a renovation recently, so the pools, common areas, and guest rooms are current rather than dated. With a 6-year-old and a not-quite-3-year-old in tow, the property was built with exactly that age range in mind: there's a water park with slides sized for kids, six outdoor pools spread across the grounds so you're rarely fighting a crowd for a lounge chair, and three swim-up bars if the adults want a drink without leaving anyone unsupervised. Seven bars and lounges total means the evening scene has options beyond the main restaurant. A spa and sauna are on-site for anyone who wants a couple of quiet hours — that's booked and paid separately from the room rate.
